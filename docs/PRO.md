# Pro and Support

Maintainer: Priyanshu Kumar Thakur

## Why a paid option exists here

Engagement teams must preserve and verify evidence over time, where support and reporting packs have professional value. The free version below stays complete for local use; nothing in the free core is removed or crippled to force an upgrade.

## Free (this repository)

- Tamper-evident local evidence ledger
- Artifact registration and verification
- Markdown, HTML and JSON reports
- Offline verification

## Paid / supported option

- Engagement evidence-pack templates
- Priority support for verification workflows
- Team handover and reporting guidance

Paid features are offered as support, setup help, templates, or a hosted service where stated. They are not hidden gates inside the free code in this repository.
